Study Tracker

Helping students put together a study plan that fits them.

Four-person group project · Design for User Interaction · Year 1, Semester 2

About this project

Study Tracker was a group project with four people. I worked on the personalised study-plan screens in Figma, from choosing a goal to reviewing the finished plan. I also created the sitemap to show how the different parts of the app connect.

What I worked on

  • Designed the screens for choosing a goal, subject, study time, learning style and intensity.
  • Created the plan summary and screens for editing, exporting and saving plans, along with progress and priority views.
  • Linked my screens with clickable interactions and created the app’s sitemap.

The idea

The idea was to help students turn their study preferences into a plan, one choice at a time.

How I put it together

One choice at a time

I gave each choice its own screen so the planning process could be followed in smaller steps. The summary brings those choices back together.

Thinking beyond the first plan

I also made screens for editing a plan, saving it and exporting it. I wanted to show the parts of the experience that come after the initial setup.

Connecting the screens

I added clickable links in Figma so the screens could be explored as a flow. These show the app idea rather than a working plan-generation system.

How it turned out

My part of the project includes 17 frames labelled with my name and the app sitemap. The gallery brings together all six screens from goal selection to the plan summary, followed by the sitemap.

Reflection

This project gave me a chance to think about a whole journey rather than just one screen. Choosing a goal, subject and study time might seem like separate tasks, but they all need to make sense together when the student reaches the summary. Working on these screens helped me practise keeping the layout and wording connected throughout the flow.

The sitemap was another way of looking at the same project. Instead of focusing on the details of each screen, I could see where the planning flow sat within the rest of the app. Together, the screens and sitemap show two sides of my work: arranging the smaller details and keeping track of the bigger picture.
