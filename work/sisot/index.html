Sistem Online Time

One of my first projects connecting webpages with a database.

Individual school assignment · Computer Science · Form 4–5, 2022–2023

About this project

Sistem Online Time was my Computer Science assignment in Form 4 and Form 5 in Malaysia, before I joined Singapore Polytechnic. The website includes watch comparisons and pages for staff, categories and products.

What I worked on

  • Worked on pages connected to staff, category and product information in the database.
  • The project includes pages and code for searching, editing, deleting and importing product information.

The idea

This assignment was an introduction to making a website with PHP and MySQL, using watches and product information as its theme.

How I put it together

Connecting pages to information

The website includes category and product selections, as well as forms for entering staff and product details.

Giving the site its own look

Watch images, a turquoise banner and Malay navigation give the pages a shared theme.

How it turned out

Reflection

This project is part of where my interest in computing began. It introduced me to the connection between a webpage and the information stored behind it. Working with PHP and MySQL was different from making a page that only displayed text and images, because the project also involved forms and database records.

I have kept it in my portfolio as an early piece of my learning journey. The design and structure belong to my secondary-school work, but they give some context to the projects I later worked on at polytechnic. It shows the starting point from which I continued learning about websites, programming and databases.
