My Pet World

A pet-care game where a little attention goes a long way.

Individual project · Back End Development · Year 2, Semester 1

About this project

My Pet World is a virtual pet game I worked on for Back End Development. Players can create pets, feed and groom them, and keep track of their care. I built on the starter setup provided for the module.

What I worked on

  • Connected the pages to the back end for accounts and pet management.
  • Worked on feeding, grooming, daily care limits, points and player ranks.
  • Included login, registration, profile pages and light and dark themes.

The idea

I worked on a game where players could look after their pets and see how each care action changes their pets’ stats.

How I put it together

Keeping pet care easy to follow

Each pet card puts happiness, health and grooming beside the Feed and Groom buttons, along with the remaining daily care actions.

Making the pages work with the data

I connected the HTML, CSS and JavaScript pages to the Node.js back end and database, using the module’s starter setup.

Adding a reason to return

Each pet has three care actions a day. A one-time reward is available when all three stats reach at least 80, and account points contribute to the player’s rank.

Reflection

This project brought the front end and back end together in a way that was easy to see. A Feed or Groom button is only a small part of the page, but behind it are changes to pet stats, saved data and the daily care allowance. Working on the game gave me practice connecting those pieces so an action on screen had a meaning beyond the button itself.

It also gave me something more playful to build. The pet cards, points and ranks gave the project a personality while the care rules kept it structured. Looking at it as a whole, it is a useful example of how my interest in interfaces connects with what I am learning about programming and databases.
