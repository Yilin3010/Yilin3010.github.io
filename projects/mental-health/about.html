What is Mental Health?

Mental health is a crucial aspect of our overall well-being that encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social health. It affects how we think, feel, and act, influencing the way we handle stress, relate to others, and make daily decisions. Mental health plays a key role in our ability to maintain relationships, perform well in school or work, and enjoy life. When our mental health is strong, we are more resilient, better able to cope with life’s challenges, and more likely to experience a sense of purpose and fulfillment. It is important to remember that mental health is not just the absence of mental illness—it also includes the presence of positive characteristics like emotional balance, self-esteem, and the ability to manage emotions and relationships. Caring for our mental health is essential at every stage of life, from childhood to old age, and it requires awareness, support, and healthy habits.
