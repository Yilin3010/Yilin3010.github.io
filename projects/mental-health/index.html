Quick Mental Health Tips
  • Take deep breaths to calm your mind.
  • Stay physically active regularly.
  • Connect with friends and family.
  • Take breaks when feeling overwhelmed.
  • Practice gratitude daily.
Where to Find Help

If you or someone you know needs immediate support, here are some resources you can contact:

  • National Mental Health Helpline: 6389 2222
  • Local Counseling Centers: Visit our About page for details.
  • Emergency Services: Call 999 for urgent assistance.
