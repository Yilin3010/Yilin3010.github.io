My Goals

I want to live a zero-waste lifestyle by making simple, practical changes. I aim to:

  • Switch to reusable containers and bags
  • Cut down on single-use plastics
  • Compost my food waste at home
  • Buy in bulk and avoid excess packaging

Overview Table

Goal Challenge Solution
Use reusable containers and bags Forget to bring them Set reminders and always keep them ready
Cut down single-use plastics Convenience of disposables Find reusable alternatives for daily use
Compost food waste No compost bin at home Join a local composting initiative
Buy in bulk Limited bulk shops nearby Plan ahead and support eco-friendly stores
